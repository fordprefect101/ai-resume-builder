What matters in most kinds of work, so you know which facts to look for:

- The work: what was done, for whom, and why it mattered.
- The person's own part, as opposed to the team's.
- Methods, tools and systems used, in the person's own wording.
- Size: how many people, customers, items, projects, or how much money or time.
- Results: what changed afterwards, including plain statements with no numbers.

Good facts are small and checkable. Not facts: opinions about quality, feelings, and anything the story only implies.
